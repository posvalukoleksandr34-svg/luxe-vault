# Owner approval and signature checklist

Production readiness audit, 2026-10-24. Companion to
[PRODUCTION_READINESS_REPORT.md](PRODUCTION_READINESS_REPORT.md), which holds
the evidence behind each item.

Every item below needs at least one of the following:
- a person's decision;
- a signature or a written confirmation;
- access this audit did not have: provider dashboards, production secrets, the
  production database, or the live site (network access to it was refused
  from the audit environment).

Nothing on this list was done by the audit.

**Who signs what.** Only two kinds of item need a signature or a written
statement from someone other than the owner:
- a lawyer's written opinion (OA-01);
- the payment providers' written confirmation (OA-02).

Every other item is the owner's own approval, decision or manual
confirmation. No compliance certificate or legal form is required by
anything in this repository, and none is invented here.

**Status values:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| OPEN | Nobody has acted yet. |
| READY FOR REVIEW | The audit's evidence is in; the owner only needs to look and approve. |
| APPROVED | The owner approved it. Set this only after the owner says so. |
| BLOCKED | Needs something that is not available yet; the item says what. |
