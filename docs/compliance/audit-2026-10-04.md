# Compliance, accessibility and SEO audit — 2026-10-04

Covers privacy and data protection, consumer law, accessibility, SEO and the
security of the new code. The security hardening before this is in
[docs/security/hardening-2026-09-29.md](../security/hardening-2026-09-29.md).

This is an engineering audit, not legal advice. Everything marked
**[OPERATOR]** needs information or a decision only the shop owner has.
The legal pages keep their "legal review required" banner until a lawyer has
read them.

## 0. The finding that outranks the rest — trade marks (CRITICAL, legal)

The shop sells "premium replicas" and uses real designers' names to describe
them: the product `brand` field and the terms' wording, *"Brand names are used
solely to describe cut and design"*.

In Switzerland (MSchG) and the EU, selling goods that copy another's
registered marks or get-up can infringe those rights **whatever the
disclosure**. Saying "replica" does not make it lawful.

The code is honest about it, and that must stay:
- the disclosures;
- the replica badge;
- structured data that names Luxe Vault, not the designer, as the brand.

Not fixable in code. **[OPERATOR] have a Swiss IP lawyer review the
catalogue before anything else in this list.**

Related, not established as infringing, needs the same review:
- the gold serif "LV" monogram, `public/brand/`, in a luxury-fashion context;
- the SEO keyword "replica sneakers" in `app/layout.tsx`.

## 1. Findings and what was done

Severity is for the shop as it runs today.

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH | **Access keys leaked to analytics.** With consent, GA4 and the Meta Pixel received full page URLs, including a support ticket's `?t=`, the unsubscribe token, the cart-reminder links and Stripe's `payment_intent_client_secret`. | `lib/sensitive-url.ts`. Vendors never load or send on, or after, such a page. GA gets scrubbed `page_location`/`page_referrer`. Those pages send `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin`. Logs (CSP reports, crash reports) are scrubbed too. |
| 2 | HIGH | **Withdrawal blocked by a checkbox.** A return could not be filed, even for a defective item, without certifying "unworn, original tags". EU law allows only a value reduction, and the refund policy already said so. | Checkbox removed (UI and schema). A note explains the possible reduction and links the policy. |
| 3 | HIGH | **Import VAT and duties not disclosed before payment.** The terms said they are not included; the checkout said nothing. | A notice under shipping whenever the delivery country is not CH/LI. Also added to the delivery help text. |
| 4 | HIGH | **Privacy Policy did not match the system.** It named Netlify (the site runs on Vercel and Cloudflare). It omitted Gemini, GA4/Meta by name, Turnstile, NOWPayments, address lookup (Photon / Google Places), the newsletter, cart reminders, the referral cookie, reviews and backups. | Rewritten (RU authoritative, EN, IT) from the code. Missing facts are visible placeholders (§3). |
| 5 | HIGH | **No account deletion**, though the policy promised erasure. | Account → Settings → "Delete my account" (`app/api/account/delete`). Deletes the Stripe customer and its cards, the newsletter row, alerts, waitlist, cart reminders, store reviews, then the auth user, which cascades to the rest. Orders, returns and tickets are kept, detached. |
| 6 | HIGH | **Newsletter without double opt-in.** Anyone could subscribe anyone, and the API revealed who was subscribed. | Migration 0051: `pending` → confirmation email → a button on `/newsletter/confirm`. One answer for every address. The admin cannot activate a pending address. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 30 days. |
| 7 | HIGH | **Cart reminders without consent.** The address was captured as soon as it was typed. | An unticked "Email me one reminder" box. Nothing is stored without it, enforced on the server. Unticking deletes the stored copy. |
| 8 | MEDIUM | **Retention periods promised but never enforced.** | `lib/server/retention.ts` in the daily sweep: tickets after 24 months, orders after 5 years, unconfirmed sign-ups after 30 days, finished cart records after 30 days. |
| 9 | MEDIUM | **Cookie consent never expired**, though the policy said 12 months. | It does now (`lib/cookie-consent.ts`). |
| 10 | MEDIUM | **Date of birth collected and never used.** | Field and API removed. Migration 0050 drops the column and its values. |
| 11 | MEDIUM | **No point-of-collection notices** in sign-up, support, reviews, stock alerts, waitlist or the AI chat; the checkout had no links to the terms or policies. | `components/privacy-notice.tsx` in each form, saying what that form's data is for. The checkout links terms, privacy and refunds above the button. |
| 12 | MEDIUM | **Order button.** "Bestellen" / "Confirmer" suggested a binding order; it only creates a cancellable, unpaid one. | "Continue to payment" (all languages). "Pay now" is the binding step, and the terms (§5) now say so. The terms also no longer claim that an account is required. |
| 13 | MEDIUM | **Unverifiable social proof.** Help text claimed "an average rating of 4.9 out of 5"; anyone-can-post store reviews were labelled "Voices of our clients". | Figure removed. A verification disclosure (EU Omnibus) and a neutral heading. Product reviews really are delivered-orders-only (`can_review`; since 2026-10-05 `review_eligibility` plus a trigger that also checks the order contains the product, one review per customer per product — see [verified-buyer-reviews](../security/verified-buyer-reviews-2026-10-05.md)). |
| 14 | MEDIUM | **Contradictory help texts.** "Inspected at our warehouse", "refund within 5 days", "must have tags". | Rewritten to match the refund policy. |
| 15 | MEDIUM | **Zoom disabled** (`user-scalable=no`, `touch-action: pan-x pan-y`), WCAG 1.4.4. | Zoom allowed. `touch-action: manipulation`. 16px form text on iOS only, to avoid auto-zoom. |
| 16 | MEDIUM | **Keyboard: drawers.** The cart, account and support drawers had no focus trap and did not return focus. The phone menu had no `aria-expanded` and no Escape. The skip link left focus on `<body>`. | `lib/use-focus-trap.ts`; disclosure ARIA and Escape on the menu; the skip link focuses `<main>`. |
| 17 | MEDIUM | **SEO: home page had no `<title>`** in any language (an explicit `title: undefined` in `pageMetadata`). | Only set when present. |
| 18 | MEDIUM | **SEO: canonicals, hreflang and the sitemap named the bare domain**, which 308-redirects to `www`. | `SITE_ORIGIN` defaults to `https://www.luxe-vault.store`; breadcrumb JSON-LD uses it too. |
| 19 | LOW | **Unsupported claims.** "Mirror Quality" on the social card; "Hurry!" urgency copy. | Replaced with "Shipped from Switzerland"; stock count stated plainly (it is real stock). |
| 20 | LOW | **Invalid ARIA** (search `aria-controls` → missing id). | Only while the panel exists. |
| 21 | LOW | **Weak keyboard focus line** (1px gold, ~2.6:1). | 2px in the text colour, for keyboard focus only. |
| 22 | LOW | **Fonts:** OFL 1.1 requires the licence with redistributed files. | `public/fonts/LICENSE.txt`. |
| 23 | LOW | **Customer email in a log line** (restock email failure). | Logs the product instead. |
| 24 | LOW | **Reference ("old") prices** are free text with no price history. | Admin guidance under the field (EU 98/6/EC art. 6a; PBV art. 16). Enforcing it needs price history; see §4. |
| 25 | LOW | Hard-coded Russian "Войти" in the five-language account panel; robots.txt allowed personal pages. | Translated. `/account`, `/checkout`, `/support/tickets/`, `/newsletter/` disallowed (all are also noindex). |

Also added: a separate **Cookie Policy** (`/legal/cookies`), listing every cookie and
localStorage key, linked from the banner, the footer and the sitemap.

Reviewed and left as they are, because they are correct:
- **Cookie banner:** Accept and Reject have equal weight; closing counts as reject; nothing optional is pre-ticked; vendors are named; withdrawal deletes their cookies.
- **Unsubscribe:** one-click `List-Unsubscribe`, POST only; a link in every email.
- **Payment:** card data never reaches the shop.
- **AI assistant:** order tool results carry no address or contact details; messages are not stored.
- **Measurements:** the fit advisor's measurements stay on the device.
- **Alt text:** informative images are named; decorative ones are `alt=""`.
- **Colour contrast:** axe reports none failing.
- **Low-stock counts:** real stock.

## 3. [OPERATOR] — information and decisions only you have

1. **Controller identity:** your full name and postal address in the Privacy Policy §1, and in the Terms. Swiss UWG art. 3(1)(s) requires them for online sales; "on request" is not enough.
2. **EU representative** (GDPR art. 27), or a lawyer's confirmation that you do not need one.
3. **Supabase project region**, and the **backup storage provider** (Privacy Policy §4).
4. **How long you keep the Telegram order slips** (Privacy Policy §5). They carry full shipping details by your decision. Delete them after dispatch, or switch the slip to order number + admin link.
5. **Apply migrations 0050 and 0051.**
   - **0051 must be applied before or with this deploy**, or newsletter sign-ups are refused.
   - 0050 drops the birth dates for good.
6. **Gemini API on a paid tier.** On Google's free tier, prompts — your customers' chat messages — may be used to improve Google's products.
7. **Existing newsletter subscribers** joined without double opt-in (`confirmed_at` is empty). Decide whether to ask them to confirm.
8. **"Private sale" framing.** A web shop with payments, a newsletter, referrals and a staff console is likely commercial. That changes warranty, VAT and identification duties. This is a question for your lawyer.
9. **Crypto refunds:** the refund policy says money goes back "by the same method". State how a crypto payment is refunded.
